Optimizers
==========

Available Optimizers
--------------------

COBYLA
~~~~~~

- Derivative-free optimization
- Good for noisy objective functions
- Recommended for VQE on real hardware

SPSA
~~~~

- Stochastic gradient approximation
- Resistant to noise
- Suitable for quantum hardware

L-BFGS-B
~~~~~~~~

- Gradient-based method
- Faster convergence on simulators
- Requires gradient information
