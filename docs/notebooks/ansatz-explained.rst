Ansatz Explained
================

The UCCSD (Unitary Coupled Cluster with Singles and Doubles) ansatz is used to represent the molecular wavefunction.

What is an Ansatz?
-------------------

An ansatz is an educated guess for the form of a mathematical function. In VQE, the ansatz is a parameterized quantum circuit.

UCCSD Theory
------------

The Unitary Coupled Cluster ansatz applies exponential operators to the Hartree-Fock state to capture electron correlation effects.
